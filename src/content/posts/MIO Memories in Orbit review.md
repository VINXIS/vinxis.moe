Didn't wanna post this on steam cuz they don't have a neutral/sideways thumb review option, which I HAVE to give it. It can't be a thumbs up or down


This has been the most conflicting experience I've ever hard when playing a game. Last time I had a conflicting experience anywhere remotely similar to this was Rain World.

Unlike Rain World tho, that had such absurd high highs but also some massive low lows. For MIO i can't rly say the same. The experience is definitely worse; if I had to use a 10-rating scale, then maybe it's like a 5? Tbh i probably can't even give it any other number because of how my experience went with this game.

---

Every single aspect on this game has me going wow this is good but also it's also so bad, like
**The music?** It's got some bangers, it's also got some terrible shit in it
**The platforming?** Sometimes it's so well designed, sometimes it's probably the most unintuitive un-thought out platforming ever
**The combat?** Sometimes it feels like ur epic as shit and feels impactful and nice, sometimes it feels like ur fighting with the controller and nothing is listening to u and the fighting just feels fucking awful
**The bosses?** Sometimes the choreography and ideas are so interesting and fun to interact with. Sometimes ur just throwing shit at a fucking wall for no reason

Also im shocked as to how much shit u can miss in this game EVEN if u do the true ending. Which is CRAZY.

Speaking of which, the normal ending probably takes u like 10-15 hours? The true ending probably takes u 15-20 hours. This is all excluding optional shit ull stumble upon

If there was anything that was consistently well done, it was 2 things:
art direction
story

very beautiful game and the story and lore were very cool; HOWWWWEVERRRRRR, at some point the optional stuff for more lore REALLY doesn't matter much in terms of how much information it really gives u

Every single session I had while playing this game I went "wow this is fucking awful... wow this is fucking amazing... wow this is absolute horseshit... wow this is so heat" it's like everything needed to be paired with some epic and terrible shit

In FACT at some point there was such amazing platforming in the game that I was so hyped to play for, but then the game decided it hates itself I guess and made it so annoying after a certain point.

Also speaking of which, I had waited EVER SO PATIENTLY for the health system in this game to be justified AT ALL. IT IS NEVER JUSTIFIED. WHY are there no healing mechanics in this game? It's just so artificially difficult and annoying as fuck for no reason really. u end up wasitn g SO much fucking time on certain things like platforming sections and stuff. There is some really difficult platforming and boss fights in this game mind u, and when it comes to platforming, it becomes like a fucking gauntlet and ur effectively like UNCONSENSUALLY doing a celeste golden strawberry just to get through that shit. and trust me i have literally gotten all the goldens in celeste TWICE.

I think most people will be way too annoyed with all the shitty system design choices of this game to actually play this, but I certainly didn't really regret spending time playing this game. As in despite all the terrible shit in this game that it's filled with, I think the game respected my time enough for me to not think it's a terrible game, and the dripfeeding of epic cocomelon bullshit thru the game that i was eating amongst the sea of shit was satiating enough sometimes
